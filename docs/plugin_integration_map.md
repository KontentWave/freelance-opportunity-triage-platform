### Known acceptance boundary

Actual frozen-corpus counts are now measured under separately authorized operator exceptions: **20/20 pass, minimum 1,086 and maximum 1,127**, with DEMO-001 reused rather than recounted. Earlier substituted-contract and faked-HTTP tests are still only offline behavior evidence, not the source of those measurements. The implemented counting guard now accepts either verified-zero-charge evidence or explicit owner risk acceptance, with separate provenance and unchanged disabled defaults. Accepted risk does not verify provider policy or guarantee a bound on unknown counting fees. A future corpus change needs new approval/hash/version and a review of the independent hard-requirement predicates. The manifest is trusted server content, never a request-selected file.

A response settled on a different UTC day from its last dispatch authorization remains uncertain even with measured usage: suspension or connection establishment may have moved actual dispatch across midnight. Evidence-referenced reconciliation must explicitly supply the verified dispatch day; that day's budget row is created and locked before run/item/attempt state, and no new provider work follows reconciliation. This avoids assigning a delayed request to the previous day's spending allowance.

UI/report rendering/download are now implemented locally in 6.3 as mapped below; owner delivery and live/release acceptance remain 6.4. No shared documents, host settings or real external data were changed.
